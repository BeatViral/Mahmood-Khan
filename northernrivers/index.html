AUDIENCE & PROMOTION

Built for the room.
Designed to travel beyond it.

Two audiences: people in the room, and those reached through filmed performances, digital content and media.

Venue capacities, ticket targets and projected attendance will be confirmed for each concert before partner commitments are finalised.

Mahmood’s previous work, advertising or relationships include Foxtel Arts, SBS, Channel 7, Channel 9/NBN, Network 10 and Aurora Community Channel on Foxtel, together with regional radio including Paradise FM and BayFM.

Season One is re-engaging those relationships and opening new television, radio and digital media conversations to explore coverage, interviews and wider reach. No current media partner is confirmed, and no future coverage or partnership is assumed or guaranteed.

Planned promotion around each concert will include:

  • local event promotion across the Northern Rivers
  • digital and social campaign content
  • professionally filmed performance clips and short-form content
  • partner acknowledgement and agreed partner content
  • local and national media outreach
  • regional radio outreach
  • venue and community promotion
  • behind-the-scenes and artist storytelling
  • ongoing content released after each concert, extending the life of the event and partnership beyond the room

Audience projections and a specific promotional plan will be developed for each concert once the venue and date are confirmed.
