01 / WHAT ARE WE BUILDING?

One season.
A whole body of work.

Live performances + an original YouTube series + music films + a documentary + television and digital promotion. A complete production and audience-development cycle.

01

Live Northern Rivers productions

Professionally produced performances, envisaged in Ballina, Byron Bay and Mullumbimby. Local musicians, singers, crews, venues and suppliers wherever practical.

02

A local band & creative team

Paid professional work — from rehearsals and arrangements to sound, cameras, lighting and editing. People receive cheques because Season One happens.

03

An original YouTube series

Music, conversations and stories behind songs. Meet the musicians, regional personalities, venues and businesses that give the Northern Rivers its character.

04

Individual performance films

Major performances become professionally mixed and edited music videos, ready to release through Mahmood’s existing channels.

05

A documentary, from day one

Follow the idea, the founding ten, band formation, rehearsals, local suppliers, promotion, performances and release. Developed for potential Australian and international film submissions; selection is not guaranteed.

WORLD POP / BORN AND LAUNCHED IN BALLINA

Our own Northern Rivers identity — a genre and a continuing music series growing from the place Mahmood calls home. This existing Ballina launch film, including Channel 9’s involvement, shows where it began.

03 / PROMOTION IS PART OF THE PRODUCT

Create it properly.
Film it properly.
Promote it properly.

Season One funds television advertising, digital promotion and continuing audience development to introduce the music and films to people who do not already know Mahmood.

Exposure is part of the production strategy. Build the audience here, release the work through existing worldwide channels, and establish a foundation for wider Australian touring.

Media history & audience planning

Previous work, advertising or relationships include Foxtel Arts, SBS, Channel 7, Channel 9/NBN, Network 10 and Aurora Community Channel on Foxtel, plus Paradise FM and BayFM.

Those relationships are being re-approached alongside new television, radio and digital conversations. No current media partner is confirmed. Coverage, audience numbers and future partnerships are not guaranteed.

Venue capacity, attendance projections and a specific promotional plan will be developed for each confirmed concert.

04 / LOCAL WORK. GLOBAL WINDOW.

A region where entertainment
is created — and sent out into the world.

Mahmood’s music already reaches international audiences. This Northern Rivers work will join that existing ecosystem.

A local guitarist’s performance can be watched in London.

A filmmaker’s work can be seen in Los Angeles.

A venue, technician or business can have a lasting place in the credits and story.

Worldwide release creates access, rather than a guarantee of viewers, broadcast placement or commercial returns.
