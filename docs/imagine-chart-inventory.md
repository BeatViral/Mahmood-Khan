## Caption decisions and uncertainties

- Billboard categories are Classical Crossover Albums or World Digital Song Sales, never a generic Hot 100 claim.
- AIR is the Australian Independent Record Labels Association; these images are not labelled ARIA.
- iTunes World / Worldwide / Músicas del mundo is a genre label, not evidence of a global aggregate ranking.
- iTunes territories use filenames where no storefront country is visible; that basis is explained in viewer notes. No total-country statistic is claimed.
- Screenshot computer dates are identified as capture dates, not official chart dates. Ambiguous numeric dates stay as shown.
- `back to back 3 songs in the top ten itunes usa.jpg` shows Sydney / Go On / Jagamarra at 23 / 24 / 25, not Top 10.
- `jahan tu nahi number 5 itunes usa.jpg` shows No. 3.
- `Ginoo top 5 on itunes usa.jpg` shows No. 3.
- `go on top 5 on air charts.jpg` shows No. 2.
- `blue snow top 20.jpg` shows No. 14; `here and now top ten.jpg` shows No. 3; `my own little bed top ten.jpg` shows No. 4.
- Google Play screenshot shows Tere Baghair in the fourth visible tile but no numbered rank or category heading. Included as an album listing, with no numerical ranking claimed.
- ARIA image arrived during implementation: Like the River, No. 1, Top 50 Physical Singles, 12 October 2009; artist credit MKF. Included as image 52.
- The public count is 43 distinct archive images, not 43 unique chart appearances: different snapshots and multiple entries may overlap.

## Maintenance

Edit `assets/data/charts.json`, then run `node scripts/build-chart-gallery.cjs`. It regenerates the static gallery and embedded viewer data. Originals already copied to assets/images/charts are sufficient for rebuilding when root originals are unavailable.
