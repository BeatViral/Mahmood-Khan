WITH ORCHESTRAS OF THE WORLD

A global recording project bringing Mahmood Khan’s catalogue of hit and No. 1 songs together with selected orchestras around the world.

Proven repertoire

A handpicked collection of Mahmood Khan’s No. 1 and Top 10 songs will form the repertoire for Orchestras of the World.

These are original songs written by Mahmood Khan with a proven chart and audience history — now being reimagined through the individual sound, character and musical identity of each participating orchestra.

Championing the songwriter

In an era of generative AI, original songwriting matters more than ever.

The project celebrates the human songwriter — the individual voice, imagination, lived experience and craft behind a song.

By bringing proven original songs into new orchestral settings, it gives existing works a new life while creating a platform for new compositions, new collaborations and the continued evolution of songwriting as an art form.

The vision

One songwriter. A catalogue of proven songs. Distinctive orchestras around the world.

Each collaboration becomes its own chapter — not simply an orchestra accompanying an artist, but an orchestra leaving its own permanent musical signature on the repertoire.

Now developing international orchestral collaborations for 2026–27.

07 / THE VOCAL JOURNEY

An extraordinary
vocal journey

Mahmood Khan’s vocal journey began with training under Seth Riggs, before he came under the mentorship of Nusrat Fateh Ali Khan. During this remarkable chapter, Mahmood recorded and produced an album with Nusrat.

The album went on to sell more than six million copies worldwide, introducing Mahmood’s work to world music radio audiences across the United States and earning him Artist of the Month recognition on MTV Asia. It was a collaboration that crossed cultural boundaries and brought listeners together through music.
