A proven orchestral story

Mahmood Khan has spent decades writing, recording and performing with orchestral musicians. His collaboration with the Willoughby Symphony Orchestra reached No. 1 on Billboard’s Classical Crossover chart.

Mahmood Khan with Orchestras of the World is the next chapter: new recordings created with distinguished orchestras in different countries, each bringing its own identity to Mahmood’s catalogue.
