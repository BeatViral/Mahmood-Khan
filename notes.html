03 / ALBUM NOTES

ALBUM
NOTES

In a world becoming louder, faster and harder to feel, it has become critical to remember what we are standing inside of: a beautiful living planet filled with mystery, imagination and endless possibility.

We were not built only to consume and rush. We were built to notice, to wonder and to rejoice.

This album comes with a story—not as an escape from reality, but as a way back into it. It is a reminder that freedom is not only a human dream. It is a natural force moving through every living thing.

If you are like me, you probably enjoy the randomness of life. But when you look closely enough, it does not seem random at all.

It was this movement—from something appearing random to revealing a deeper connection—that inspired me to write this album.

Our lives are brief. We are fading observers, like rays of sunlight repeatedly soaking into the earth before moving towards different pastures.

Life is strange, infectious and true—a mirror that favours the skilled, creates beauty from astonishment and feeds on inspiration.

This is Power Lines.

— Mahmood Khan
