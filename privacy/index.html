PRIVACY / YOUR CHOICES

Privacy & analytics.

Updated 4 October 2026

This page explains data use across the Mahmood Khan website, including the IMAGINE campaign, presented by Mahmood Matloob / Beat Viral Music. For privacy enquiries, contact mahmoodkhanteam@gmail.com.

Analytics is your choice

This website uses Google Analytics 4 only after you accept analytics, including a choice you previously saved. Before acceptance, or if you decline, our Google Analytics tag does not load and tracked clicks are not sent to it. You can use the whole page without accepting.

After acceptance, analytics helps us understand visits, referring websites and campaign links, device and browser information, approximate location, and which partnership or contact buttons visitors select. Campaign links may include source labels such as an outdoor poster or email campaign. Google Analytics uses cookies, including _ga and related _ga_ cookies, to distinguish browsers and sessions. These clicks indicate interest; they do not tell us whether an email was sent or a partnership agreed.

Our custom click events do not include email messages or visitor-entered details. Advertising consent remains denied, and Google signals and ad personalisation signals are disabled in the website’s tag configuration. Google processes analytics data under its own service terms; processing may take place outside the UK. Read Google’s Privacy Policy and its UK analytics privacy information.

Remembering and changing your choice

We save your Accept or Decline choice and its date in your browser’s local storage for 180 days, solely to remember your preference across pages on this website. After that, we ask again. Clearing browser storage may also reset this choice. The 180 days applies to this preference, not to the retention period of data already collected by Google.

Use Analytics choices in the footer of any page to change your decision. Withdrawing consent stops our analytics, clears its first-party GA cookies and reloads the page to unload Google’s analytics script. Withdrawal does not automatically erase data previously collected.

Hosting, fonts and videos

GitHub Pages hosts this website. Delivering a page involves technical connection information such as your IP address and browser details. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

The website loads fonts from Google Fonts and embeds YouTube videos using YouTube’s privacy-enhanced domain. Loading these resources or interacting with a video connects your browser to Google services, which receive technical information needed to deliver them. These resources are separate from our optional GA4 analytics; declining analytics does not block the fonts or embedded videos. Their handling of data is described in Google’s Privacy Policy. This privacy page uses the same optional analytics and consent controls, and contains no embedded media.

Contacting us

Partnership links open your email application. If you send an enquiry, we receive your email address, message and any information you choose to include, and use them to respond and discuss the project. The campaign has no account registration or payment form.

To ask about your information, request access, correction or deletion, or raise a privacy concern, email mahmoodkhanteam@gmail.com. UK visitors may also contact the Information Commissioner’s Office.
