Mahmood Khan is a singer-songwriter, musician and producer whose work has crossed countries, cultures and musical forms.

From his early years learning piano and guitar, to recording and producing in Los Angeles, working with Nusrat Fateh Ali Khan, creating albums in India and recording live at the Sydney Opera House, his life has been shaped by movement, reinvention and an enduring belief in the power of songs.

Power Lines continues that journey.
