01 / THE ARTISTIC QUESTION

Same songwriter.
Same songs.
Same harmony and form.
Different orchestra.

What emerges that was
never written down?

Four songs form the common repertoire. Each orchestra brings its own musical personality, interpretation and cultural character.

The song, harmony and form remain fixed. Orchestral colour and controlled improvisational freedom create space for each ensemble to reveal its own identity.

04 / THE PROPOSED UK CHAPTER

Two cities.
Two musical identities.

The proposed UK chapter brings the project to Manchester and London, with Manchester Camerata in development and the Royal Philharmonic Orchestra proposed for the London chapter.

Recording sessions. Captured on film.

Mahmood and each orchestra will record the same four songs in studio sessions. Those sessions will be professionally filmed to create finished recordings, performance films and behind-the-scenes material for release.

This is an invitation to help fund the recordings and films. No concert tickets are being offered through this campaign.
