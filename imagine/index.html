CHAPTER / MANCHESTER01

Manchester
Camerata

In development

Manchester roots. An adventurous musical spirit.

Founded in 1972, Manchester Camerata brings chamber music’s intimacy to a wide musical world. Under Music Director Gábor Takács-Nagy, its work spans Mozart, collaborations with Martha Argerich, and projects with New Order, Aziz Ibrahim and Lewis Capaldi. Opening Glastonbury for 40,000 people reflects its adventurous spirit.

That belief in music’s power to connect people also drives Music in Mind, its pioneering programme for people living with dementia. Working with University of Manchester researchers, Camerata has become the UK’s first Centre of Excellence for Music and Dementia. Artistic exploration and human connection are woven into its identity.

Explore Manchester Camerata ↗

The Manchester chapter is being developed with Manchester Camerata following direct discussions around orchestra lineup, rehearsal, studio and filming.

CHAPTER / LONDON02

Royal Philharmonic
Orchestra

Proposed London orchestra

A great orchestral tradition. A world of musical possibilities.

Founded by Sir Thomas Beecham in 1946, the Royal Philharmonic Orchestra combines an international reputation with musical versatility. By 1949, it had made more than 200 recordings for major labels. Today, under Music Director Vasily Petrenko, its repertoire spans symphonic works, film scores, pop and video games.

Collaborations with Yehudi Menuhin, Shirley Bassey, Rod Stewart and Kylie Minogue reflect its musical breadth. Alongside international touring and performances at London’s major concert halls, its RPO Resound programme brings music into communities. A distinguished recording heritage and an enduring appetite for discovery continue to shape its identity.
