Same songwriter.
Same songs.
Same harmony and form.
Different orchestra.

Four songs form the common repertoire. Each orchestra brings its own musical personality, interpretation and cultural character.

The song, harmony and form remain fixed. Orchestral colour and controlled improvisational freedom create space for each ensemble to reveal its own identity.
